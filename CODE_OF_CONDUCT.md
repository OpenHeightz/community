# Open Heightz Community Code of Conduct

**Version 1.0 | Effective 15 October 2026**

**Technology Belongs to Everyone.**

Open Heightz is committed to providing a respectful, inclusive, supportive and professional environment for everyone participating in our community.

These standards apply to the founder, organization members, maintainers, moderators, contributors and visitors.

## 1. Expected Behaviour

All community members are expected to:

- Treat others with dignity, fairness and respect.
- Welcome beginners and support constructive learning.
- Respect diverse experiences, backgrounds and abilities.
- Provide helpful feedback and criticism without personal attacks.
- Acknowledge contributors and respect intellectual-property rights.
- Protect confidential information, personal data and account credentials.
- Keep discussions relevant, constructive and appropriate.

## 2. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following conduct is prohibited:

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation or threats.
- Hate speech, personal attacks or targeted bullying.
- Sharing private information without authorization.
- Spam, fraud, impersonation or deceptive promotional activity.
- Plagiarism or deliberately misrepresenting another person's work.
- Malicious disruption of projects, discussions or collaboration.
- Retaliation against anyone who raises a genuine concern.

Respectful disagreements, technical criticism and constructive debate are welcome.

## 3. Reporting Violations

Concerns about misconduct may be reported privately to:

**malulekaclesh@gmail.com**

**Email subject:** Open Heightz Conduct Report

Include a description of the concern and any relevant links or evidence.

Do not publicly expose sensitive personal information when reporting a violation.

If a complaint involves the person receiving these reports, participants may use GitHub's reporting tools instead.

## 4. Moderation and Enforcement

Authorized Open Heightz moderators may:

- Provide guidance or issue warnings.
- Request changes to inappropriate content.
- Hide, remove, close or lock discussions.
- Restrict participation temporarily.
- Permanently ban participants for serious or repeated violations.
- Escalate appropriate cases through GitHub's reporting mechanisms.

Moderation should be fair, proportionate, consistent and reasonably confidential.

Serious violations may result in immediate action.

## 5. Scope

This policy applies to official Open Heightz repositories, discussions, issues, pull requests and community activities.

It also applies when individuals are formally representing Open Heightz in external spaces.

## 6. Community Responsibility

Every participant contributes to the culture of Open Heightz.

We encourage members to share knowledge, ask questions, recognize contributions and resolve disagreements responsibly.
